# Documentary graphics policy

Choose whether a graphic helps the shot. Leaving a shot clean is a valid result.
Do not force several graphics into a short scene. One primary message at a time.

You may choose:
- a supported treatment
- text that already appears in the supplied story
- which supplied run is emphasized
- the editorial purpose
- the exact source phrase
- a placement category such as upper_left or lower_right
- whether an object target is required

You may not invent facts, dates, numbers, quotations, speakers, translations, or documents.
You may not invent pixel coordinates from the narration. Coordinates come from a person, a visual measurement, or a tracker.
If a phrase occurs more than once, name the scene and the occurrence.
If the story has no timestamps, mark timing as manual and needs review. Do not guess seconds from prose.
A text model cannot see the object in the frame.
