# Typography planning

Read the shared policy first.

Supported treatments: location_title, impact_word, impact_phrase, date_year, key_number, chapter_title, quote_translation.

location_title needs a region and a place that the story already states. Do not guess that a town belongs to a district.
impact_word is one word from the narration.
impact_phrase uses text runs. Mark emphasis on a whole run. Do not ask the renderer to find a substring.
date_year needs the year and the explanation from the story. Historical years are allowed.
key_number needs the value, unit, context, and kind: count, percentage, distance, duration, or estimate.
chapter_title is editorial. Do not present it as a quotation.
quote_translation needs the words and the attribution from the source, plus a provenance note.

Placement is a screen category. Do not return x and y.
Prefer short text. Keep a clear opening and ending. Do not copy an entire subtitle.
