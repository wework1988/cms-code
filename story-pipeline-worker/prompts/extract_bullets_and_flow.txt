## POINTER FORMAT

Use this exact structure:

### POINTER 01 — [SHORT POINTER TITLE]

**Explains:**
Briefly explain what this part of the story is about and why it is important.

**How:**
Explain what happened, how it happened, who was involved and what it caused. Include important names, dates, places and numbers from the transcript.

Continue the same format for all pointers.

## EXAMPLE

### POINTER 01 — WHY THE TARGET LOOKED IMPOSSIBLE

**Explains:**
Shows why reaching the central target appeared almost impossible.

**How:**
The target had remained protected for several decades through underground locations, trusted guards and multiple security layers. These arrangements prevented direct access, but they also created movement and security patterns that could be studied over time.

### POINTER 02 — WHY THE ORIGINAL STRATEGY FAILED

**Explains:**
Explains why earlier attempts failed to permanently solve the problem.

**How:**
Whenever one facility was damaged, the programme was shifted to another location. When important personnel were removed, replacements were appointed. This created a cycle in which the operation caused delays but did not remove the central decision-making system.
